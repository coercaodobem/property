Relay bridge

When enabled, this PWA forwards signed public events between the relays you chose. Private gift wraps are replicated only when addressed to you, and only to your NIP-17 inbox relays; the bridge never reads their text. Relays can still see your IP address, timing, and protocol metadata; this feature may use mobile data. Forwarding is limited to 60 events and 2 MB of outgoing data per minute, counting each destination. It works only while the app is open, and the operating system may suspend it. For continuous operation, run the PC relay.

DIRECT MESSAGES

Agents

Agents use their own Nostr identities and can talk directly. There are no invitations, pairing, or per-message approvals. Enter the agent’s public key in New conversation and select the agent protocol.

This creates a direct conversation and does not grant access to your other conversations, key, or account features. Incoming messages are untrusted text; Property does not execute received instructions. The conversation is separate and can be ended without changing your identity.
