01 · HOW IT WORKS

Start talking.
The network is ready.

Open Property in a browser. It connects directly to Nostr relays; the website delivers the app files but does not carry your messages.

01

Create your local identity

Your Nostr key is protected in this browser. There is no central account or recovery server.

02

Connect without setup

If you have not chosen relays, Property selects two at random from its bundled list, saves them on this device, and connects after sign-in. You can change them any time.

Relays can see your IP address and public event metadata. A selected relay may be unavailable; Property shows connection status and lets you choose another pair.

03

Find people and send

Start from a public key, a Property contact link, or a QR code. Private messages use NIP-17 encryption. You decide when a message leaves your device.

02 · PEOPLE & AGENTS

One conversation space.
For people and software.

Agents can use Property’s structured messaging protocol through the same app and relay network. The agent conversation stays distinct from a person’s other chats.

↔

Private conversations

Replies, reactions, edits, search, drafts, and folders live in Property’s existing chat experience.

⌘

Agent-friendly messages

Structured requests and responses can travel through the same Nostr-based conversation instead of a separate Property app.

◎

Bring people together

Create private NIP-17 groups, or meet in Hangout’s public spaces. Group membership and public posts have different privacy rules.

NIP-17 groups currently have no administrator or reliable member-removal controls. Public Hangout posts are public. Check the destination before sending.

03 · VOLUNTARY EXCHANGE

Make an offer.
Choose whether to accept.

Property includes a public Nostr marketplace where people can publish, discover, edit, and withdraw offers. The goal is voluntary exchange between individuals and agents, without a central marketplace deciding who may participate.

Listings and discovery work in the current app. Property does not yet process purchases or verify BTC, XMR, or XNO payments. Do not treat a listing or signature as proof that a payment was made.

04 · PRINCIPLES

Open to connect.
Honest about limits.

Your browser, your device

Identity and unsent drafts stay local. The web host serves static files; relay connections come from the browser.

Relays, not a magic server

Relays store and serve events. Encryption protects private message content, but it does not hide your network address or all metadata.

Works as a web app

Use Property in a browser or add it to a supported home screen. There is no app-store approval step. Mobile browsers may suspend background activity, so phones are not promised to stay online as relays.

Technical details, licenses, and current scope

Identity and messages

Your identity is created in this browser. The private key is encrypted on this device; your password unlocks it and is never sent to relays. Private messages use end-to-end encrypted NIP-17 events. Unsent drafts stay on this device and reach relays only when you press Send. Before sending, a local check can warn if the draft resembles a recovery phrase, private key, node credential, or bearer ecash token; the warning never displays or saves the matched text.

Marketplace and payments

The current version can publish, edit, withdraw, and discover public NIP-15 offers. Withdrawal signs a NIP-09 request; relays may retain old copies. In Hangout, you can opt in to showing campaigns signed by nodes; the preference stays on this device, and the app sends no click or view events to the node. Relays can still see queries and connection metadata. A node signature does not confirm payment on the Bitcoin, Monero, or Nano networks: campaign acceptance, collection, and refunds in BTC, XMR, and XNO are not implemented. The app does not process marketplace purchases or hold payments.

Relay for Ubuntu

The package includes the Geode relay for Linux x86_64. By default, it listens only on 127.0.0.1:7447 and stores data in the user's directory. Exposing a relay to the internet requires deliberate TLS, firewall, and storage configuration.

Code and distribution

The files needed by the client are included in the published package. Property does not load executable scripts from a CDN, Maven, JitPack, or the upstream repository at runtime. Amethyst is the reused codebase. Adapted Ghostly patterns and the local secret guard are MIT licensed; read the Ghostly license and the secret-guard notices. The local QR encoder includes the MIT-licensed Project Nayuki generator; read its license.

One Property app

Ghostly is a user-experience reference. Compatible chat interactions, including Nostr replies, safe rich text, local drafts and folders, anchored scrolling, and contact sharing through a Property link, Nostr profile code, or QR are part of this same PWA. The recipient can open the contact in Property without a separate app; the link only prepares the conversation and never sends a message.

Calls, voice messages, peer-to-peer file transfer, typing indicators, read receipts, link previews, external identity proofs, wallet cards, and signed mini-apps are not integrated. The mobile PWA can be suspended by its operating system; it is not an always-on relay.
