ABOUT THE PROJECT

Communication under your control.

Property is a Nostr client that connects you directly to the relays you choose. The website serves the app package; messages do not have to pass through a central server.

Identity and messages

Your identity is created in this browser. The private key is encrypted on this device; your password unlocks it and is never sent to relays. Private messages use end-to-end encrypted NIP-17 events.

Relays and mobile operation

You choose the relays and can install the app from a browser at an HTTPS address. The voluntary relay bridge works while the page is active. Mobile operating systems may suspend the browser, so a phone is not advertised as an always-on relay.

Marketplace and payments

The current version can publish, edit, withdraw, and discover public NIP-15 offers. Withdrawal signs a NIP-09 request; relays may retain old copies. In Hangout, you can opt in to showing campaigns signed by nodes; the preference stays on this device, and the app sends no click or view events to the node. Relays can still see queries and connection metadata. A node signature does not confirm payment on the Bitcoin, Monero, or Nano networks: campaign acceptance, collection, and refunds in BTC, XMR, and XNO are not implemented. The app does not process marketplace purchases or hold payments.

Relay for Ubuntu

The package includes the Geode relay for Linux x86_64. By default, it listens only on 127.0.0.1:7447 and stores data in the user's directory. Exposing a relay to the internet requires deliberate TLS, firewall, and storage configuration.

Code and distribution

The files needed by the client are included in the published package. Property does not load executable scripts from a CDN, Maven, JitPack, or the upstream repository at runtime. Amethyst is the reused codebase; see the license included in the repository.
